One app. Nine ways to grow.

Less “where do I start?”
More “let’s try.”

From your first spoken sentence to your next favourite word, find a way to practise that feels like you.

01 Say it out loud

Your voice.
A little more
confidence.

Go from knowing the sentence to saying it. Record yourself speaking Norwegian, see your words transcribed, and get feedback on your pronunciation.

  1. 01
    Record & discover

    Speak a sentence. Read its transcription and explore your pronunciation feedback.

  2. 02
    Replay & listen

    Hear your own recording and notice how you sound.

  3. 03
    Compare & practise

    Listen to the native-speaker version, compare it with yours, and give it another go.

02 A word worth keeping

Look it up.
Make it yours.

A new word doesn’t have to stay a mystery. Search the built-in dictionary, find its translation to your language, and add the word and translation straight to your own flashcard collection.

One simple connection between “what does that mean?” and “I remember this.”

  1. Look up
  2. Save
  3. Practise

03 A little friendly competition

Think fast.
Find the words.
Beat the clock.

Sixteen letters. How many Norwegian words can you spot? Search a 4 × 4 letter grid against the clock and compete with other players to find more valid words before time runs out.

A fun way to stretch your vocabulary, sharpen word recognition, and put what you know into play.

↗ Multiplayer. Timed rounds. Norwegian words.
