Effective Date: 06.22.2024

Welcome to Learn Norwegian, the mobile application designed to help you learn the Norwegian language. Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. We collect information in the following ways:

Information You Provide: This includes information necessary to enhance your experience, such as creating an account, subscribing to our service, participating in our community, or when you contact us directly.

Device Information: We may collect information about the device you use to access our app, including the hardware model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information.

Usage Data: Information on how you use our app, such as the features accessed, the time spent on the app, and other statistics.

User Content: Information included in the flashcards you create, the profile pictures you upload, and any other user-generated content.

The information we collect is used to:

We may share information as follows:

Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your information may be transferred. We will provide notice before your personal information is transferred and becomes subject to a different Privacy Policy.

4. Your Rights

You have certain rights regarding the personal information we hold about you. These may include the right to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information. You can typically manage your information directly within your account settings or by contacting us directly.

5. Security
